G2 HUD · Phone MVP

Uses your phone's GPS and compass / motion to run the real HUD — no glasses. In Handheld mode, hold the phone up and pan it around: nearby airports appear where they actually are. Tap the screen to lock the one under the reticle. Best in landscape.

You'll be asked to allow Location and Motion access.

Experimental / supplemental only. Not a certified instrument, not for operational use. GPS altitude is geometric (not flight level). Compass/motion from a phone are approximate.
